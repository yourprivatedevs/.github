Your Private Dev is a one-person senior engineering service that builds workflow automation, AI agents, internal tools and AI platforms. One direct builder, fixed-scope written quotes, full code ownership at handover. Fully remote, working with clients worldwide.

## What gets built

- **Workflow automation.** Replace repetitive manual work with stable systems: invoicing, reporting, document and data-extraction workflows for finance and operations teams.
- **AI agents and integrations.** Practical AI inside existing systems: agents that read the request, check your systems and carry the task through.
- **Custom internal tools.** Ops dashboards, CRMs and back-office workflows shaped around how your company actually works.
- **MVP development.** Idea to a first production release, with a clean handover.
- **HubSpot and ERP integrations.** Custom HubSpot CRM cards and data syncs, Business Central invoice automation, and two-way syncs between business systems.

Client work is private to the client. The repositories below are small, generic libraries for problems that come up in this kind of work.

## Open source

| Repository | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [installment-split](https://github.com/yourprivatedevs/installment-split) | Split a money total into installments, cent-accurate: equal or by percentage, in integer minor units, for 0, 2 and 3 decimal currencies. |
| [sync-loop-guard](https://github.com/yourprivatedevs/sync-loop-guard) | Stop echo loops in two-way syncs between two systems, with origin tagging, content-hash comparison and a short suppression window. |

Both are TypeScript, have no runtime dependencies, and are MIT licensed.
